# MorseReader Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

MorseReader does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal data.

The app uses the device camera to detect and decode signals from LEDs. Camera images are processed locally on the device and are not transmitted to any server or retained by the developer.

Decoded messages and app settings may be stored locally on the device. This information is not accessible to the developer and is removed when the app is deleted.

MorseReader contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or third-party data-collection services.
